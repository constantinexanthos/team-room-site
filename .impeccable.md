## Design Context

### Users and purpose
Developers installing a small Claude Code plugin. Team Room lets Claude and
Codex / ChatGPT deliberate together and return one structured brief.

### Creator's preferred direction
Use the original website as the reference: plain white background, black
text, the heading “Team Room,” one short plugin description, installation
commands, and the original informational sections. Keep a moderately narrow
reading column (928px including horizontal padding). Start content near the top.
The creator explicitly rejected the “Good thinking is a team sport” headline,
warm paper background, added taglines, scene labels, and excess decoration.

### Design principles
- Keep the original copy and simple hierarchy. This is a plugin, not a large
  marketing proposition. Do not add slogans, eyebrows, or meta descriptions.
- Use actual Claude and ChatGPT logos inline where the model dots used to be.
  Model names are ordinary inline text with the same baseline, font, and line height
  as the paragraph; icons must not lift their text through an inline-flex baseline.
- Keep the robot scene open and unframed, on the same white page.
- Robots stay EXCLUSIVELY in the left and right margins. No center meetings
  or empty scene lanes above/between content. The center is for reading.
- Desks and computers face their seated agent directly: center the screen and
  keyboard on the worker, with the desk front edge facing the chair.
- Every robot has an assigned workstation, visibly works there, walks around
  furniture to talk to a partner, then returns to that same desk. No unassigned
  resting points or wandering into empty space.
- Use brisk walks (roughly six seconds per trip), steady bodies, and small foot
  movements. No bouncing or whole-body jumps. Match typing and terminal activity
  to the time the worker is actually at the desk.
- Dialogue should be short, concrete engineering exchanges: APIs, hooks, retries,
  database indexes. Avoid vague motivational lines.
- Hide decorative side scenes when there is insufficient screen margin.
- Desks stay stationary at the sides only. No central tables, plants, or
  furniture in the reading column. Robot paths must remain clear of desk bounds.
- Preserve responsive behavior, pause/resume, and reduced-motion support.
